About

This web app was create by me Gabriel Holmquist, to enhance my character creation and gameplay experience of Cyberpunk 2020 the Table Top Role Playing Game (TTRPG). While I love this game those either un initiated into TTRPG specifically Path Finder and Dungeons and Dragons, might find Cyberpunks character creation confusing or irritating. My goal with this site was to make an easier way for specifically my friends and those new to the paper versions of cyberpunk to immerse themselves in my option one of the most engaging character creation systems.

Getting Started

To get started I would recommend you have either a paper or electronic copy of the Cyberpunk 2020 handbook. I own both and can vouch for your local game store or amazon of a paper copy, or DriveThruRpg for a digital copy.
